Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how VERITAS CONSULTING collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with our website, including when you:

  • Submit an enquiry through our contact form
  • Request a consultation or professional engagement
  • Communicate with us via email or telephone
  • Provide your name, organisation, email address, phone number, area of interest, and message/requirement

This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and to facilitate professional communication regarding our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries, provide requested services, and communicate with you regarding professional engagements.
  • Improve our website and service offerings
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Secure data transmission protocols
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security assessments and monitoring
  • Employee awareness and training on data protection

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website currently does not use cookies, behavioural tracking technologies, advertising pixels, or similar technologies for analytics, profiling, targeted advertising, or tracking user behaviour.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, or safety
  • With your explicit consent

Any third-party service providers we engage are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes for which it was disclosed.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access your personal information
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete information
  • The right to request deletion of your information
  • The right to restrict or object to processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at infovc@veritasconsul.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When your information is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other factors. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
